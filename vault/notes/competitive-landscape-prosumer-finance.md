Running notes rather than the formal scan. The approved documents are linked on the
LedgerLane market research gate — this is where the observations land before they get
written up properly.

## Who is actually in the set

Four apps matter. Two are venture-funded and building for small teams, which means they
keep adding collaboration features we do not need to match. Two are solo-developer apps
that are closer to our shape and considerably better at the boring parts: export formats,
offline capture, receipt OCR accuracy.

The venture-funded pair are the ones we lose search traffic to. The solo pair are the ones
we lose users to after week two.

## Pricing, as of the August refresh

All four lead annual. Trials range from 7 to 14 days. Nobody in the set does a free tier
with a usage cap, which is interesting — either they all tried it and it failed, or nobody
has tried it. Worth a note to find out, because a capped free tier would suit the
"scan a receipt and see" moment far better than a trial does.

## What is working for them that is not obvious

- One competitor asks for the tax year at onboarding and pre-configures every category from
  it. Feels minor. It removes the single most tedious setup step in the category.
- Another does not ask for an account at all until you export. See
  [[Deferred signup keeps showing up in the funnel]].

## Sense-check cadence

Refresh this monthly. The competitive set is stable but the pricing and onboarding move
more often than you would expect, and the last two useful experiment ideas both came from
noticing a change here rather than from our own analytics.
